This site has no server, so there's no live "upload" button — instead, this form writes the recipe card for you. Fill it in and click Generate JSON. You'll get two things to add on GitHub.com: a new file to create under data/recipes/, and one line to add to data/manifest.json. The new card appears next time the page loads.

Kept card by card, recipe by recipe.
