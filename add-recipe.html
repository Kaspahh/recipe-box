This site has no server, so there's no live "upload" button — instead, this form writes the recipe file for you. Fill it in — optionally attaching a photo below — click Generate, then Download ZIP. It bundles the new recipe file, an already-updated manifest.json, and your photo (if you added one), all in the right folder structure, ready to upload straight into your repo on GitHub.com.

Kept card by card, recipe by recipe.
